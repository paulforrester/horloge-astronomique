- **Down-facing** is the area (mm²) facing down more steeply than 45° and not on the bed: overhangs *and* bridges. Under about 100 mm² it is bridged holes, seats and slots that print without support; larger values are explained in the notes. Each part is posed to keep it small.

- **Fits not final.** The press, slide and bearing fits wait for the tolerance coupons, so print `tolerance_coupons` first and regenerate after `FITS` is updated.
